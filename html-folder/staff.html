Kai - The Lost Wanderer

Community Storyweaver

Kai was one of the first polynesian explorers. He dreamed of conquering the seas since young, with his name itself meaning 'Sea' it was pretty much his destiny as far as he saw. At the age of 14 he finally set sea for the first time and rarely set foot on land after. One day however, he set out at the age of 23 only to find himself caught in a storm of unprecedented proportions. Praying to Tangaroa the Polynesian God of the Sea seemed to be of little help this time as he was swept off his va'a (boat) he figured it was his end as the world faded under the weight of the sea. Next thing he knew he was being pulled out of the sea by men on a strange metal vessal that he struggled to understand.
Being an ancient polynesian seafarer Kai has seen many things in his time. This, along with his own stories at the sea meant Kai was a man of stories heard of only in legend. One of the men who happened to be on the boat that rescued him heard his story and despite the disbelief over his story, recommended him to the Museum of Magic. Here he was able to draw upon his ancient knowledge to replace the missing knowledge lost over time. He became one of the foremost experts on putting together what ancient cultures were truly like, making him invaluable to the Museum.

Prof. Thorne Blackwell

Anthropologist

Born with knowledge of a language long thought dead Thorne always struggled as a child. With his knowledge he found it difficult to learn his native swedish toungue. This lead to many labelling him as slow in his youger years, making it difficult to interact with those his age. Eventually he started taking solace not in humans but in animals, specifically a stray cat he found. Naming the cat Rose due to the pattern on her back he would often run to rural areas to wonder in whatever nature he could find. Nature always seemed to calm him and it always welcomed his presence until the day it didn't. Thorne and Rose ran into a wolf who would injure Rose. Running to the nearest buildings with a bleeding Rose Thorne had no idea what to do. Was he about to lose the only being he called a friend? Thats what he thought and every time the thought ran through his head he felt like he lost more of himself until finally he found people, one of which being a nurse.
He wasn't even able to choke out a word before she picked up the injured Rose and started tending to her, and managed to save her. That was the day he started pursuing a career in medicine with the hope he would never have to go through that again. Here he would learn the language that seemed to be carved into his very soul was a language of flesh. Using this he quickly became one of the best. Eventually he found the "Museum of Magic" where he would first be hired as a mundane doctor before his talents quickly shone through leading to an anthropologist asking for help on a study. Afterwards he would be recruited into the Anthropology department.

Selene Vire

Archaeologist

Selene grow up fascinated by the endless possibilities around her. While others spent their time playing she was planning out her own experiments. Any attempts of IQ testing ended up void as she would either score perfectly, or not at all. Along with her recluse nature this made her seek out science and knowledge to an even greater extent. When she was 12 it finally paid off as she officially became the youngest researcher to present a truly groundbreaking discovery, shocking the science world. That discovery was a base energy outside of convential physics flowing not just through rocks or trees, but everything from humans to insects.
Not long after her discovery the entire scientific community fought to see who would get her on their team. After yet another day of being pulled in every direction she would meet a elderly lady. At first she was just going to be polite and leave as soon as possible until she saw something she believed impossible. The elderly lady could manipulate that energy she discovered, a supposedly impossible task according to her research. Immediatly she started to pester the woman asking more questions than she ever thought she had. The lady not on for giving out her secrets merely left a buisness card with the name "Museum of Magic" before vanishing as if she was never there.
